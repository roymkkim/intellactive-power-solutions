Opportunity

We set the standard a decade ago. But critical challenges remain beyond the reach of today’s pack- and module-level BMS. Now, we’re working with SwRI and NuEnergy to prove what cell-level BMS can do.

  1. Thermal runaway

    Today’s BMS cannot see a fire coming, and it cannot stop one. Recent costs: GM, $2.0B; Moss Landing, ~$730M.

    We isolate the cell before onset. One cell fails; the pack stays up.

  2. Battery life

    Today’s BMS cannot tell when cells are wearing out.

    Up to 40% more capacity retained and about 20% less maintenance, at half the BMS cost.

  3. Integration

    Today’s systems are custom. Every new cell type means a redesign.

    A conventional BMS takes about 60% longer to install.

  4. Cybersecurity

    Today’s BMS cannot prove which cell sent a reading or refuse a bad command.

    Forged commands are rejected at the cell.
