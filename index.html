The opportunity

Ninety-eight percent still
run a ten-year-old BMS.

Most battery storage systems use the same standard BMS. It has three gaps.

  1. 01

    Safety

    Today’s BMS cannot see a battery fire coming, and it cannot stop one. Ours detects the early warning signs in a single cell and isolates that cell before a fire starts.

  2. 02

    Lifetime

    Today’s BMS cannot tell when batteries are wearing out. Ours tracks the health of every cell, so operators fix problems before they happen and get more years from every battery.

  3. 03

    Integration

    Today’s systems are custom-built, so every change means a redesign. Ours is plug-and-play. It lives on each cell, sets itself up, and works with any battery type.

This matters most where failure is not an option: backup power for AI data centers, medical, and the grid. That is a $500 million starting market, growing more than thirty-three percent a year.

Business model

We sell the BMS.
Manufacturers build the rest.

We design the hardware and software. A contract manufacturer builds the boards. We load and test the software in our own facility, then sell the BMS to battery storage manufacturers.

Every manufacturer uses the same standard BMS, so they are stuck competing on price. We give them what their competitors do not have: better safety, longer life, and faster integration — at less than half the all-in cost of a standard BMS for mission-critical use.
