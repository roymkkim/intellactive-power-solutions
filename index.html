Opportunity

We set the standard a decade ago. But critical challenges remain beyond the reach of today’s pack- and module-level BMS.

IntellActive cell-level BMS is an innovative, robust, and flexible solution to today’s challenges.

  1. Thermal runaway

    Today’s BMS cannot see a fire coming, and it cannot stop one.

    We isolate the cell before onset. One cell fails; the pack stays up.

  2. Battery life

    Today’s BMS cannot accurately track battery degradation.

    We self-calibrate and actively reconfigure SOC and SOH during operation to achieve predictive maintenance.

  3. Integration

    Today’s systems are custom. Every new cell type and application means a redesign.

    We self-configure across chemistries and capacities through software, without hardware changes.

  4. Cybersecurity

    Today’s BMS have authentication bypass and command injection vulnerabilities.

    We have the architecture to prevent forged cell data and unauthorized commands through cell-level authentication and operating limits.
